Monograph 01

Thermodynamics of Avian Pyrogenesis

Unlike earthly combustion that creates carbon char, the Phoenix cycle operates via catalytic resinous fusion. Plumes synthesize frankincense sap into crystalline filaments capable of enduring 1,400°C without cellular dissipation.

Monograph 02

Abyssal Barometric Gigantism

The Kraken’s circulatory mechanics rely on hemocyanin complexes pressurized under 3,000 atmospheres. When ascending toward oceanic shoals, gas pocket expansion creates the illusion of a rising volcanic reef.

Monograph 03

Preservation of Non-Corporeal Relics

Relics from creature encounters—such as molted flame quills or chitinous suction rings—are preserved in airtight bell jars lined with sandstone dust, myrrh oil, and sea sponge to prevent ontological decay.

Plate IV · Archival Provenance

Department of Curatorial Sciences

Conserving the Memory of What Never Was

The Myth Atlas is an ongoing academic effort to catalog the living folklore of terrestrial and abyssal organisms. Every 3D specimen in this digital cabinet has been modeled in strict adherence to primary historical accounts, medieval bestiaries, and sailor oaths.

Our conservators employ photogrammetric recreation methods alongside historical text analysis, restoring color, scale, and motion to creatures once relegated to marginalia and myths.
